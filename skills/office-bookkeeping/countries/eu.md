# European Union (common rules for all 27 member states)

> Last checked: 2026-11-13. Read this together with the member state's own file (e.g. `mt.md`). Not tax advice.

## Full VAT invoice: required fields (VAT Directive 2006/112/EC, Art. 226)
1. Date of issue
2. Unique sequential number (one or more series)
3. Supplier's VAT number
4. Customer's VAT number when the customer accounts for the VAT (reverse charge) or receives an intra-EU supply of goods
5. Full name and address of supplier and customer
6. Quantity and nature of goods, or extent and nature of services
7. Date of supply (or payment on account), if different from the issue date
8. Taxable amount per rate or exemption, unit price excluding VAT, and any discounts
9. VAT rate(s)
10. VAT amount payable
11. If exempt: reference to the exemption provision
12. If the customer pays the VAT: the words **"Reverse charge"**
13. Where applicable: "Self-billing", "Cash accounting", margin scheme wording, tax representative details, new means of transport particulars

## Simplified invoices (Art. 220a, 226b)
- Allowed up to EUR 100 (a member state may allow up to EUR 400), and for exempt small enterprises.
- Not allowed for intra-EU supplies of goods or cross-border reverse-charge supplies.
- Minimum: issue date, supplier identity, type of goods/services, VAT amount (or the data to calculate it).

## Credit notes
Any document that amends an invoice counts as an invoice. It must refer to the original invoice specifically and unambiguously (Art. 219).

## Time limits
Intra-EU supplies of goods and cross-border reverse-charge services must be invoiced by the 15th day of the month after the supply (Art. 222). Other deadlines are set nationally.

## Cross-border checks
- B2B supplies to another EU country: check the customer's VAT number on **VIES** (https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/vies/) and keep proof of the check (date + result).
- Services bought from another EU country are usually reverse-charged by the buyer: the supplier's invoice shows no VAT and says "Reverse charge".

## E-invoicing (ViDA, Directive (EU) 2025/516)
- Since 2025, member states may require domestic e-invoicing without EU approval.
- **1 Jan 2028**: platforms for short-term accommodation and passenger transport can become deemed suppliers (member states may delay to 2030).
- **1 Jul 2030**: structured e-invoices (EN 16931) mandatory for intra-EU B2B, issued within 10 days, with near real-time reporting. The EC Sales List is abolished.

## Checks the skill must run
- All Art. 226 fields present on a full invoice; simplified invoice only when within limits and not cross-border.
- "Reverse charge" wording and the customer's VAT number present when the supplier charged no VAT on cross-border B2B.
- VAT numbers start with the country prefix (e.g. MT, IT, DE; Greece uses EL).
- Credit note references the original invoice number and date.
