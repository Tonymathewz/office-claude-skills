# Improve system

A weekly review of your Claude setup: what got done, what you had to correct, and what to fix. It works well as a scheduled task on Mondays and Fridays.

## Your setup (edit this once)

- **Your standing rules:** `<e.g. never send emails for me; print in black and white; preview before changing anything>`
- **Save reports to:** `<YOUR REVIEWS FOLDER>`
- **Review period:** `<7>` days

## 1. Gather (cheaply)
- **Skills:** list your own skills and read each SKILL.md. Skip the built-in ones.
- **Notes:** read your saved preferences and any project notes changed since the last review.
- **Recent work:** list the chats in the review period. Open a chat only where it shows a correction, a repeated procedure or an unfinished item.

## 2. Audit checklist
- **Overlap:** two skills that trigger on the same words or cover the same job.
- **Stale facts:** past dates, old devices, files or folders that have moved.
- **Rule conflicts:** a skill that breaks one of your standing rules.
- **Missing skills:** any procedure done 3 or more times without a skill.
- **Repeated corrections:** anything you had to say twice. That becomes a candidate rule.
- **Weak descriptions:** missing the words you actually use, so the skill doesn't trigger.
- **Bloat:** long skills that could be shorter (they cost more to run).

## 3. Report
Save `System Review <YYYY-MM-DD>.md` in your reviews folder with three sections:
1. **Recap:** what got done, grouped by project, one line each.
2. **Findings:** ranked most important first, each with its evidence (the chat or skill name).
3. **Proposed fixes:** exact wording.

## 4. Apply only with a yes
List every change and apply it only after the user approves. Never delete a skill, file or note unless the user explicitly asks.
